Trees connected by the same mycelial network can share carbon, water, and
warning signals — a stressed tree can pass distress signals to its
neighbors through the fungal web between their roots.

Older "mother trees" tend to be the most connected hubs, funneling extra
resources to seedlings growing in their shade, including their own
offspring, which they seem to somehow recognize.

It's not quite a nervous system, but it's not *not* one either.
