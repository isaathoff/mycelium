Fungi are one of the few organisms that can break down lignin — the tough
stuff that makes wood rigid. Without them, dead trees would basically
never fully break down, and forests would slowly bury themselves in their
own debris.

Every fallen branch you've ever stepped over was, or will be, mycelium's
lunch. The nutrients it releases go straight back into the soil network
described in [Mycelial Networks](#card-mycelium-networks).
