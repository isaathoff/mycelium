Trees communicate with each other—but not directly. Just like in a game of “telephone” with a can, they need a “string.”

This “string” is a fungus: not the cap we see on top, but a network of thread-like cells underground that connects root to root.

Through this network—the mycelium—trees exchange water, nutrients, and signals.
