Mycelium is the root-like web of thread-thin fungal cells that spreads
through soil, wood, and leaf litter — mostly invisible, doing most of the
work.

A single mushroom is just the fruiting body. The real organism is the
network underneath: sometimes a few square meters, sometimes acres wide,
quietly trading nutrients between trees that never touch.

**Why this app is named after it:** a mycelial network doesn't have one
center. Every node connects to whatever's nearby and useful. That's the
shape this whole card collection is trying to have — trees, people,
memories, and ideas, cross-linked instead of filed away in folders.
